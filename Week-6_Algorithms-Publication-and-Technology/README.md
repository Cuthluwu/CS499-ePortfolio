For the emerging-technology part of the module, I looked at a possible future direction for the Weight Tracker: importing measurements from a Bluetooth-enabled scale and giving the user optional progress feedback.

The journal discusses Bluetooth scale data, Android permissions, accessibility, user-controlled feedback, and some of the privacy and ownership questions that would have to be handled before adding the feature.

These are ideas for a later version. The current Weight Tracker does not connect to a physical scale, and I do not present Bluetooth syncing or the behavior-feedback design as completed features.
