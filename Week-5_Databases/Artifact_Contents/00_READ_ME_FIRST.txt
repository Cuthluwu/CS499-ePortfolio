FORWARD-LOOKING PRODUCT ROADMAP
03_DATABASE_DOCUMENTATION/BEHAVIOR_DESIGN_AND_BLUETOOTH_ROADMAP.md describes a proposed
goal-aware green, restrained-red, and neutral feedback system plus Bluetooth smart-scale
synchronization. It is supported by behavioral, accessibility, Android, Bluetooth, and product
sources. The roadmap is not presented as completed physical-device interoperability; it defines
the architecture, privacy controls, database extensions, and acceptance evidence required before
that claim could be made.
